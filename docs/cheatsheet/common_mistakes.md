# Common Mistakes & Invalidation Checklist

> **The top 6 fatal errors traders make with the Fractal Model and how to eliminate them.**

---

## 1. Entering on a Wick Instead of a Candle Body Close

!!! danger "The Fatal Error"
    A candle wicks through the blue CISD line, and the trader clicks "Buy Market" while the candle is still actively moving. The candle then immediately retracts and closes below the line, trapping the trader.

- **The Rule**: You must wait for the countdown timer to reach `0:00`.
- **The Axiom**: *"Wicks do the damage (sweeps); bodies tell the truth (delivery)."*

---

## 2. Fighting the Higher Timeframe Bias

- If the Daily and 4-Hour candles are in aggressive bearish delivery (creating lower lows and lower highs), **do not trade bullish 1-minute reversals** unless price has tapped a significant HTF Point of Interest.
- Always check the HTF Candle Projection cluster on the right side of your chart before entering.

---

## 3. Widening Your Stop Loss Beyond C2

- In the Fractal Model, the lowest point of the C2 manipulation wick (`c2_low` for bullish, `c2_high` for bearish) is the **absolute invalidation barrier**.
- If price violates C2 by even 1 tick, the entire setup premise has failed.
- **Never move your stop loss further away**. Take the predefined loss and wait for the next setup.

---

## 4. Chasing the C3 Candle (FOMO)

- When C3 opens and expands rapidly, retail traders experience extreme FOMO and enter at the highest point of the candle.
- Institutions will then initiate a pullback to retest the 50% equilibrium or CISD line, stopping out late buyers.
- **The Rule**: If you missed the Model 1 CISD breakout close or the Model 2 T-Spot limit, **let C3 close** and look for a **Model 4 continuation entry on C4**!

---

## 5. Overtrading Outside Institutional Killzones

- The Fractal Model requires institutional volume to achieve directional displacement.
- Trading during the dead lunch hour (12:00 – 13:30 NY Time) or late afternoon post-settlement leads to choppy ranges and false breakouts.
- Stick strictly to **London Open (02:00–05:00 NY)** and **New York AM (08:30–11:00 NY)**.

---

## 6. Pre-Flight Execution Checklist

Print or keep this checklist visible during trading:

- [ ] Is price currently inside an institutional Killzone (London Open or NY AM)?
- [ ] Has C1 established a clear reference high and low?
- [ ] Has C2 swept that high/low into an HTF Point of Interest (FVG/OB)?
- [ ] Has C2 closed without body acceptance beyond the sweep level?
- [ ] Has the CISD benchmark line been clearly identified?
- [ ] Has a candle **body** closed past the CISD line?
- [ ] Is my Stop Loss mathematically anchored to the C2 extreme?
- [ ] Have I plotted my Take Profit targets at `-1.0` and `-2.0` standard deviations?
