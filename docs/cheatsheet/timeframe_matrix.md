## 2. Model Presets Explained

### 1m - 15m Model
- **Target Audience**: Index futures day traders and fast scalpers.
- **How It Works**: 
    - The 15-minute candle serves as the setup candle (C1 and C2).
    - When the 15-minute candle wicks into a level and begins reversing, you execute on the 1-minute chart.
    - Typical trade duration: **15 to 45 minutes**.

### 1m - 1H Model
- **Target Audience**: Forex London session traders (EURUSD, GBPUSD).
- **How It Works**:
    - The 1-hour candle serves as the setup (C1 and C2).
    - For example, the 02:00–03:00 1-hour candle forms the C2 sweep of Asian low.
    - As the 03:00 1-hour candle opens, you execute on the 1-minute chart.
    - Typical trade duration: **45 to 90 minutes**.

### 15m - 4H Model
- **Target Audience**: Full-day intraday trend traders.
- **How It Works**:
    - Captures the massive 4-hour candle directional expansion.
    - Setups are less frequent but yield 100+ points on NQ and 150+ pips on Gold.
