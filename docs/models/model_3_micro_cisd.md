## 1. What You See on the Indicator

Model 3 is a **fractal inside a fractal**:

1. **Higher Timeframe Setup**: A 15-minute or 1-hour chart forms a C2 sweep and draws the shaded **T-Spot Box**.
2. **Lower Timeframe Zoom**: On your 1-minute execution chart, you observe price dipping down into that exact shaded region.
3. **Micro Fractal Cycle**: Inside the box, a 1-minute C1, C2, and C3 form in miniature!
4. **Micro CISD Line**: The indicator plots a micro blue line marking the open of the last 1-minute bearish candle.

## 2. Step-by-Step Execution Rules

### Bullish Execution:
1. **Pre-condition**: 15m candle is C3, and price has pulled back into the 15m T-Spot discount zone.
2. **Switch / Watch 1m Chart**:
    - Look for a 1-minute candle to take out a short-term micro low (purging internal sell stops).
    - Watch for an impulsive 1-minute green candle to displace upward.
3. **The Trigger**:
    - The 1-minute candle body **closes strictly above the micro CISD line**.
4. **Order Execution**:
    - Buy Market immediately upon the 1-minute candle close (or place a limit order on the micro FVG formed by that candle).
5. **Stop Loss (SL)**:
    - Placed strictly 1 tick below the **micro low formed inside the box** (often just 5–8 points on NQ!).
6. **Take Profit (TP)**:
    - Target the **Higher Timeframe standard deviation targets (`-2.0` / `-2.5`)**.
    - Since your stop is microscopic (e.g. 8 points) and the HTF target is 80 points away, this setup regularly delivers **1:10 Risk-to-Reward**!
