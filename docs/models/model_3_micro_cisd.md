## 1. What You See on the Indicator

Model 3 is an algorithmic **fractal inside a fractal**:

1. **Higher Timeframe Context**: A 15-minute or 1-hour chart forms a C2 sweep into an HTF discount POI and projects the shaded **T-Spot Box**.
2. **Lower Timeframe Zoom**: On your 1-minute execution chart, you observe price delivering down into that exact shaded region.
3. **Micro Fractal Cycle**: Inside the POI, a 1-minute C1, C2, and C3 form in miniature!
4. **Micro CISD Line**: The indicator plots a micro blue ray originating strictly from the **`Open` of the first 1-minute bearish candle** that delivered price into the micro low.

## 2. Step-by-Step Execution Rules

### Bullish Execution:
1. **Pre-condition**: 15m candle is in C3, and price has pulled back into the 15m T-Spot discount zone [20,424.00 – 20,442.00].
2. **Watch the 1m Execution Chart**:
    - Look for a 1-minute candle to take out a short-term micro low (Micro C2, purging internal sell stops).
    - Watch for an impulsive 1-minute green candle to displace upward.
3. **The Trigger**:
    - The 1-minute candle body **closes strictly above the micro CISD line** (originating at the micro benchmark candle open).
4. **Order Execution**:
    - Buy Market immediately upon the 1-minute candle close (or place a limit order on the micro FVG formed by that candle).
5. **Stop Loss (SL)**:
    - Placed strictly 1 tick below the **micro low formed inside the box** (often just 6–10 points on NQ!).
6. **Take Profit (TP)**:
    - Target the **Higher Timeframe standard deviation targets (`-2.0` / `-2.5` or HTF Buy-Side Liquidity)**.
    - Since your stop is microscopic (e.g. 15 points) and the HTF target is 60–100 points away, this setup regularly delivers **1:5 to 1:10 Risk-to-Reward**!
