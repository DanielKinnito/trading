    Model 4: C4 Trend Continuation Entry¶

    Continuation Style Anti-FOMO Framework Target: -4.0 Macro Extension

    "I was away from my desk when C2 swept and C3 exploded. How do I get into this running trend without buying the top?"

    Visual Setup Schematic¶

    Below is the photorealistic chart breaking down Model 4 execution — showing the retest of broken C1 structure and expansion into -1.0 and -2.0 Fibonacci projections:

    Figure M4.1: Model 4 execution showing shallow pullback and continuation during the C4 institutional expansion.

    1. What You See on the Indicator¶

    Model 4 is designed specifically to prevent traders from FOMO buying at the peak of an explosive expansion:

    1. C3 Closes Strong: A massive green displacement candle closes near its high, having broken above C1 high.
    2. C4 Opens: The indicator places a label C4 and projects the C4 Continuation Support Zone.
    3. Midpoint Dotted Line: Represents the 50.00% Equilibrium of the preceding C3 candle.
    4. Dashboard State: Bottom-right HUD displays C4 Trend Distribution.

    2. Step-by-Step Execution Rules¶

    Bullish Execution:¶

    1. The Mistake to Avoid:
      • Never buy at the peak of C3. Wait for C3 to close.
    2. The Pullback:
      • When C4 opens, price naturally pulls back as algorithms rebalance early longs.
      • Price dips to retest the broken C1 high (turned institutional support) or C3 Fair Value Gap.
    3. The Trigger:
      • Enter long as price tests the top of the C1 range or the 50% midpoint of C3.
      • Alternatively, enter on the close of the first 1m green candle that rejects the C4 box.
    4. Stop Loss (SL):
      • Placed 1–2 ticks below the 50% midpoint dotted line of C3 (or below the C4 pullback wick).
    5. Take Profit (TP):
      • Target the -2.0 and -4.0 Macro Standard Deviation extensions.

    4. Invalidation & Common Mistakes¶

    Do Not Trade Model 4 If C3 Wicks Extensively

    If C3 closes with a giant upper wick (indicating heavy institutional selling), do NOT trade Model 4. Model 4 is ONLY valid when C3 closed with a massive, dominant solid body!
